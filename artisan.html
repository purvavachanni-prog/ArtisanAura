Return & Refund Policy

1. Handmade Variations

Because every product on ArtisanAura is individually handcrafted by small independent artisans, slight variations in color, texture, shape, and finish are natural characteristics and signs of authentic craftsmanship.

2. Returns & Replacements

We accept return requests within 7 days of delivery only if the item received is severely damaged, defective, or incorrect.

  • Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging.
  • Custom-made or personalized artisan orders cannot be returned unless damaged upon arrival.

3. Refund Processing

Once your return is inspected and approved by our quality team, refunds will be issued to your original method of payment within 5-7 business days.
